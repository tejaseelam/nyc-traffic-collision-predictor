How the system works

One reproducible pipeline builds a complete panel for every retained cell and six-hour period—including periods with no crash.

01Open dataCrashes, weather, roads, speed limits
→
02Clean & joinValidate locations and preserve missingness
→
03500 m panelFour six-hour periods per day

05Rank & evaluateBaselines, logistic regression, LightGBM
Train2021–2022Fit model parameters
Validate2023Select and compare models
Final test2024Opened once, after choices are fixed

What is new in our approach

01

Complete panel

Every retained cell and time period is included, even when no crash occurs.

02

Past-only history

Rolling features use only earlier periods, preventing information leakage.

03

Honest integration

Missingness and match quality are retained instead of inventing unsupported values.

04

Decision-focused evaluation

Risk ranking is tied to the share of crashes captured in the highest-risk mapped areas.

Limitations & data

The explorer communicates where the current model is useful—and where the evidence remains incomplete.

35,679

Crashes without valid coordinates

These records cannot be assigned to a 500-meter cell.

73

Points outside retained land cells

Valid points can fall in cells removed by the 1% land threshold.

76.7%

Crash periods missed at top 5%

The best current model captures 23.3% of crash cell-periods in the highest-risk 5%.

1 station

Daily weather coverage

Central Park weather is applied citywide and contributes almost no current ranking information.

Additional cautions

  • Speed limits are a present-day snapshot, not a historical record for every year.
  • Exact road-hour traffic matched too few crashes, so traffic volume is deferred.
  • Published crash data may arrive after a delay; lag sensitivity is still planned.
  • Permutation importance describes global model behavior, not causes for one cell.
